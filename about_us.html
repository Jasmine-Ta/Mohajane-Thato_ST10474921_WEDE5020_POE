Our Journey & Practices

Our History

Sunrise Acres Farm is a family-owned agricultural business based in Gauteng, South Africa. We produce fresh seasonal vegetables and livestock products for households, restaurants, and local businesses.

Mission & Farming Practices

Our core mission is to become a trusted local supplier of fresh, locally sourced farm products while supporting sustainable practices with our customers & community. We are deeply committed to sustainable farming methods that protect the soil and keep our vegetables and livestock naturally vibrant and healthy.
